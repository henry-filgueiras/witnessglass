# Temper

## Goal

Preregister and execute a comparative repair experiment over candidate repair families for
`rarity_of_agreements`, against a semantic contract written before any candidate was scored — and
find out whether a simple statistic exists that repairs the two empirically relevant defects without
destroying properties already demonstrated to be useful.

## Rationale

sprint:16 measured, and this round reproduced from the same evidence, two defects that are not
hypothetical: argument-order dependence is present in **118 of 118** real candidate pairs and moves
the designated pick in **3 of 29** candidate sets; accumulation crossings are observed **13** times
across those same 29 sets, the largest at **+4.128 nats** where 10 agreements outrank 11. Both are
reachable from recordings this project actually holds.

That is a mandate to *commission repairs*, not to adopt one. The purpose of this round is not "find a
formula that passes the known tests" — a formula chosen for passing the tests it was shown is fitted
to them, and sprint:15 exists precisely because a statistic discovered that way needed adversarial
commissioning afterwards. The purpose is to state what the statistic is *supposed to mean* first,
derive candidates from that meaning, and then find out what the meaning costs.

This is also the first round conducted under decision:7. The propagation pass it mandates is run and
recorded before any trial, and it is reported as evidence about decision:7 rather than as housekeeping.

## Success criteria

- The semantic contract is written and committed before any candidate is scored, and each clause
  names the exact computed quantity that would witness its violation.
- Candidate families are derived from the contract, each with a stated interpretation independent of
  the tests it will face. A candidate that can only be justified by "it passes" is rejected.
- The feasibility pass is followed by an explicit propagation pass over every mechanism it found, and
  every criterion that mechanism touches is corrected or exempted **before** trials run.
- Every prior adversarial family runs unchanged against the frozen original and every candidate.
- Candidates are replayed against sprint:16's exact real candidate sets.
- Selection is by stated eligibility, never by aggregate pass count. Multiple survivors are preserved.
- Nothing is adopted. At most one survivor earns an adoption *experiment*.

## Non-goals

No adoption, no change to the incumbent selector, the production statistic, the representation, the
metric, or the null. No new search procedure. No threshold tuned on the corpus, and no treatment of
the corpus as ground truth. No weakening or rewriting of any prior family to accommodate a candidate.
No recording content in any artifact. Nothing pushed.
