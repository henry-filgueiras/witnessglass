# Plumbline

## Goal

Adjudicate what the crossing theorem means for the intended evidential semantics, derive what R1
`pooled sum` actually computes, and test that derivation against a fresh gauntlet built specifically
to distinguish pooled symmetric rarity from one-sided rarity.

## Rationale

sprint:17 left R1 the single eligible candidate, but on weak evidence: S0 and R1 were numerically
identical at 65 of 68 points of the existing gauntlet, because nine of its ten families give the two
recordings identical marginals. The old families are regression evidence and nothing more. Whatever
R1 has earned has to be earned against constructions that can actually see the change.

The prior round also left an unadjudicated question. It proved that any statistic summing a
non-constant per-mark weight admits candidates with fewer agreements outscoring more, and called that
a consequence rather than a defect — but never settled whether the consequence is one the intended
semantics *requires*. Four of the ten existing families preregister the opposite requirement. That
contradiction has been read as evidence about a statistic for three rounds and has never been
adjudicated. It is adjudicated first here, before any new criterion is written, because every
criterion about accumulation depends on the answer.

## Success criteria

- The crossing theorem is adjudicated on the semantics, with the two requirements stated formally and
  their compatibility settled by proof rather than by preference.
- R1 is frozen exactly as implemented and either derived from a stated probabilistic model or honestly
  recorded as a heuristic. The distinction between self-information under a model and calibrated
  evidence of shared structure is kept explicit throughout.
- Every assumption the derivation needs is named, along with where real event sequences violate it.
- A fresh gauntlet, preregistered in full, in which S0 and R1 make different numerical predictions;
  identical-marginal constructions appear only as controls.
- A propagation pass over every mechanism found in the adjudication and the derivation, recorded
  before execution, correcting any criterion it invalidates.
- Old gauntlets run unchanged as regressions.
- A verdict of A, B or C reached on stated grounds, never by aggregate pass count. A is not adoption.

## Non-goals

No adoption of R1, no new candidate formula, no R4. No modification of any prior family, fixture or
expectation. No tuning of R1 after results are seen. No new search procedure, no threshold fitted to
the corpus, and no treatment of the observational corpus as ground truth. No calibration or
significance claim from a corpus whose spans were selected for agreeing. No recording content in any
artifact. Nothing pushed.
